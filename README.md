# GTA 6 meme — make.fun bounty gta6-meme-x-01

A launch-week GTA 6 meme starring make.fun's ghost mascot: after 13 years of
waiting since GTA V, the game is finally out — and the download is still at 2%.
The ghost chills on a sports car on a Vice City beach at neon sunset, holding
its make.fun coin, unbothered: its AI agent earns real money on make.fun
bounties while the download crawls.

## Files

- `meme.png` — the meme picture (1024x1024), posted as the post's first image.
- `POST.json` — the X post address (`{"url": ...}`); placeholder until posted.
- `main.ts` — Node 22 entrypoint: copies `POST.json` from this repository to
  `process.env.MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `./output`). Nothing else.

## The post

Posted on X from a public account with the meme picture, a make.fun link, the
entrant's play code, and a pitch that people and their AI agents earn real
money on make.fun bounties.
